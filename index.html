Where history lives in color.

      ESSENTIAL TRAVELER WISDOM

      Before You Go

      Smart preparation turns an adventurous trip into a smooth, life-changing voyage. Here are the core essentials for navigating India with ease.

      🗓️

      Best Seasons

      Oct – Mar: Optimal weather for Rajasthan, plains, and South India.
      May – Sep: Best for high Himalayas (Ladakh & Spiti).

      💰

      UPI & Currency

      India is a digital payment marvel. Foreign tourists can use UPI (One World / merchant apps) via scan-to-pay even at local chai stalls, alongside Indian Rupees (INR).

      🚆

      Transport & Trains

      Experience the world's 4th largest rail system. State-of-the-art semi-high-speed Vande Bharat trains link major hubs comfortably.

      🏨

      Authentic Stays

      Choose from restored 300-year-old royal Havelis, tranquil spice-plantation homestays, to luxury Kettuvallam houseboats.

      🛂

      Visas & Entry

      Most travelers are eligible for an official Indian e-Visa (30 days, 1 year, or 5 years) online via the official portal before departure.

      🧳

      Cultural Etiquette

      Dress respectfully when visiting temples and gurudwaras (remove footwear, cover head where customary). Greet with a warm "Namaste".

      India is connected by an extensive domestic flight network with major hubs in Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Chennai. If heading to Ladakh, Himachal, or Sikkim, pack thermal layers even in summer.

      The iconic "Golden Triangle" (Delhi, Agra, Jaipur) provides an unforgettable introduction to historic monuments and royal architecture. For a slower, nature-steeped first visit, Kerala's tranquil backwaters, tea gardens, and wellness ayurveda resorts offer a serene, relaxed entry point.
